Payment links

Customers pay through Square when they check out: on their own phone they go straight to Square's checkout, and the kiosk shows a QR code to scan. The link is also in the confirmation email and PDF. Shipping is added at checkout when they choose it. Prices already include tax.

Push catalog

Turn this off if you change prices directly in Square and don't want this app to replace them. New items always get this app's price.

    Duplicates

    Finds extra copies of your designs in Square (from pushes that created a new item each time) and deletes them, keeping the copy each design is linked to. Push your designs once first so each one is linked.

    Filament inventory

    Compares the filament colors your designs use against what’s actually on the shelf in Spoolman, matched by color rather than name (the two systems never call a color the same thing). Flags colors you don’t stock or are running low on.

    API keys are set as environment variables in Portainer. Email can be set below instead.

      Kiosk mode

      For a booth tablet: open /?kiosk=1 once on that device. It hides outside shop links, uses larger buttons, and clears the cart after inactivity. Open /?kiosk=0 to turn it off.

      Email (SMTP)

      The password is stored in db.json on the data volume. Settings saved here replace the SMTP_* environment variables.

      Logo

      Shown in the store header and on order PDFs. PNG or SVG with a transparent background works best, up to 2 MB. Only PNG and JPG logos can be printed on the PDF.
